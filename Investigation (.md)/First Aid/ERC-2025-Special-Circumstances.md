# ERC-2025-Special-Circumstances

Source: https://www.erc.edu/media/wwufbysp/gl2025-06-spec-circ-e.pdf#page=43

Reference status: Summary of the ERC 2025 guidance; see the linked original.

## PDF page 43

Microgravity resuscitation. The European Resuscitation Council does not endorse a single compression technique because data from real spaceflight are insufficient.

The ERC describes airway management and defibrillation, “but only once the patient is secured”. Use the approved onboard checklist.

The document recommends: “Consult telemedicine support during cardiac arrest” in low Earth orbit if feasible and if crew are available. The most medically qualified crew member makes decisions about stopping resuscitation in consultation with remote support.
