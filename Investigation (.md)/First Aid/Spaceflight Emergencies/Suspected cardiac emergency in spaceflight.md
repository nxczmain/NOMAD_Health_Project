# Suspected cardiac emergency in spaceflight

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/appendix-e-health-and-medical-care-technical-requirements/

Reference status: Onboard training summary. NASA's risk inventory lists coronary syndrome and cardiac arrest separately.

Scenario terms: heart attack | myocardial infarction | acute coronary syndrome | chest pain | chest pressure | infarto | dolor de pecho | dolor en el pecho | presion en el pecho

## Onboard response

1. Alert the crew medical officer and activate the approved onboard medical emergency procedure for suspected acute cardiac symptoms.
2. Report symptom onset, chest discomfort, breathing and responsiveness. Report any changes and provide readings already available.
3. Keep the crewmate under observation and have the medical officer coordinate assessment and available ground consultation.
4. If the crewmate becomes unresponsive and is not breathing normally, switch to the mission cardiac-arrest checklist and use mission-trained CPR.
