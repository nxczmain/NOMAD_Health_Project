# Behavioral health crisis onboard

Source: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/5-action-steps-to-help-someone-having-thoughts-of-suicide

Reference status: Support and escalation summary for training. The mission behavioral-health procedure governs the onboard response.

Scenario terms: suicidal | suicide | self harm | hurt myself | behavioral emergency | danger to others | suicidal thoughts | suicidio | suicidarme | autolesion | hacerme dano | crisis de salud mental

## Onboard response

1. If there is immediate danger, alert the medical officer and another trusted crewmate through the approved onboard emergency channel.
2. Listen calmly and report the immediate risk in the person’s own words where possible. Help them connect with crew and medical support.
3. When safe, reduce access to hazards and stay connected until help takes over. If there is danger to others, give space and seek help from a safe position.
