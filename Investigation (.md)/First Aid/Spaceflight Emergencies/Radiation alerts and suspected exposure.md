# Radiation alerts and suspected exposure

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/radiation-protection-technical-brief-ochmo.pdf

Reference status: Spaceflight operations summary from NASA radiation protection guidance.

Scenario terms: solar particle event | solar storm | radiation alarm | radiation alert | radiation exposure | radiation sickness | acute radiation syndrome | tormenta solar | alerta de radiacion | exposicion a radiacion

## Onboard response

1. Announce a mission radiation alert and follow the approved flight or EVA contingency and shelter instructions for the vehicle.
2. Report the time, crew locations and available dosimeter readings to the crew medical officer and mission support channel.
3. Record changes in symptoms and exposure history for medical assessment. Symptoms alone do not establish a radiation dose or acute radiation syndrome.
