# Severe bleeding, trauma and burns

Source: https://www.stopthebleed.org/get-trained/

Reference status: First-aid priorities for training. Use the crew’s approved equipment, restraint and casualty movement procedure.

Scenario terms: severe bleeding | heavy bleeding | blood everywhere | hemorrhage | fracture | broken arm | broken leg | head injury | spine injury | blunt trauma | penetrating injury | electric shock | electrical injury | burn | burns | chemical burn | eye chemical | hemorragia | sangrado abundante | fractura | traumatismo | descarga electrica | quemadura

## Onboard response

1. Check for hazards to rescuers, including fire, electricity or chemical contamination. Alert the crew and medical officer when the scene is safe to approach.
2. For severe external bleeding, apply firm direct pressure when safe and consistent with crew training. Use additional bleeding-control equipment according to the approved protocol.
3. For major trauma, report the injury mechanism, location, responsiveness and breathing. Follow the crew’s approved stabilization and movement procedure.
4. For burns or chemical injury, report the agent, affected area and any breathing or eye symptoms. Follow the mission exposure or burn checklist.
