# Stroke, seizure and altered consciousness

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/

Reference status: CDC recognition and first-aid principles adapted to onboard communication and scene safety.

Scenario terms: stroke | face drooping | slurred speech | one sided weakness | seizure | convulsions | fainted | unresponsive | unconscious | wont wake up | derrame | ictus | convulsion | desmayo | inconsciente | no responde

## Onboard response

1. Alert the medical officer for abrupt speech changes, weakness, a seizure with urgent features or loss of responsiveness. Report when symptoms began or when the crewmate was last known well.
2. During a seizure, clear nearby hazards, track the time and protect the person from injury. Avoid forcibly holding their limbs or putting anything in their mouth.
3. When it is safe, reassess responsiveness and breathing. If they are unresponsive and not breathing normally, use the mission resuscitation checklist.
