# Heat, cold and metabolic emergencies

Source: https://www.erc.edu/media/wwufbysp/gl2025-06-spec-circ-e.pdf

Reference status: Recognition and escalation summary. Clinical treatment depends on the approved mission checklist and available equipment.

Scenario terms: heat stroke | heatstroke | overheating | heat illness | hypothermia | severe cold | hypoglycemia | low blood sugar | severe dehydration | golpe de calor | hipotermia | hipoglucemia | deshidratacion grave

## Onboard response

1. Alert the medical officer for severe overheating, cold exposure, symptomatic low glucose or a change in responsiveness.
2. Report recent EVA or exertion, environmental exposure, symptoms, onset and readings such as temperature or glucose if already available.
3. Stop the ongoing exposure when safe and follow the onboard checklist for cooling, warming or glucose correction. Avoid giving anything by mouth to someone unable to swallow safely.
4. If the crewmate loses responsiveness, assess breathing and follow the approved emergency and resuscitation procedure.
