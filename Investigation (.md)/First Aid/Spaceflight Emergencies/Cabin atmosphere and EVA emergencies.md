# Cabin atmosphere and EVA emergencies

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/spacestation/2019/01/15/station-trio-practices-emergency-before-radiation-physics-research/

Reference status: Training summary of NASA cabin contingency and EVA references. Vehicle-specific actions come from the approved emergency checklist.

## Onboard response

1. Announce the alarm and location to the crew. Activate the matching vehicle or suit checklist for fire, toxic release, loss of pressure or life-support failure.
2. Protect rescuers and use the vehicle’s emergency breathing, isolation or evacuation procedure as trained. Confirm that the scene is safe before approaching a casualty.
3. Notify the crew medical officer and report the alarm, exposure, compartment or suit, time of onset and affected crew.
4. For symptoms after a pressure change or EVA, report the pressure transition and symptom timing. The medical officer coordinates evaluation for decompression sickness and other causes.
