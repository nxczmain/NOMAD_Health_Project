# Airway obstruction and severe breathing difficulty

Source: https://www.erc.edu/media/i2vllpae/gl2025-12-faid-e.pdf

Reference status: ERC first-aid recognition adapted to onboard escalation. Use the mission-trained technique for the available space, gravity and equipment.

Scenario terms: choking | obstructed airway | choking on | cannot breathe | cant breathe | trouble breathing | severe asthma | wheezing | airway obstruction | atragantamiento | se atraganta | se esta ahogando | no puede respirar | asma grave

## Onboard response

1. Call the crew medical officer and activate the onboard emergency procedure for severe breathing difficulty or a suspected airway obstruction.
2. If choking is suspected, report whether the person can cough effectively, speak and breathe. Use the approved onboard choking procedure for that presentation.
3. When trained and safe, establish the support needed to perform the approved maneuver in the local gravity.
4. If responsiveness or normal breathing is lost, move to the mission resuscitation checklist.
5. When a cabin or suit alarm accompanies breathing symptoms, follow the vehicle hazard checklist and protect rescuers before providing care.
