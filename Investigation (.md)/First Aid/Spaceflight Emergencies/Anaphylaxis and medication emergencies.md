# Anaphylaxis and medication emergencies

Source: https://www.erc.edu/media/i2vllpae/gl2025-12-faid-e.pdf

Reference status: First-aid recognition and reporting summary. The approved mission protocol governs drug administration.

Scenario terms: anaphylaxis | allergic reaction | throat swelling | throat is closing | severe allergy | overdose | opioid overdose | medication reaction | anafilaxia | reaccion alergica | garganta se cierra | sobredosis | reaccion a medicamento

## Onboard response

1. Alert the crew medical officer and activate the emergency procedure if a reaction affects breathing, responsiveness or circulation, or if overdose is suspected.
2. Report the substance, exposure or drug, the amount and time if known, symptoms and changes in breathing or responsiveness.
3. Have the medical officer coordinate treatment using the approved onboard kit and checklist. Monitor responsiveness and normal breathing while help is arranged.
4. If the crewmate becomes unresponsive and is not breathing normally, switch to the approved resuscitation checklist.
