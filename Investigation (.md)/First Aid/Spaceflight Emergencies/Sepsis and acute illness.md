# Sepsis and acute illness

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/sepsis/about/index.html

Reference status: Sepsis recognition summary with onboard reporting. A clinician evaluates the cause and chooses treatment.

Scenario terms: sepsis | septic shock | infection and confusion | fever and confusion | severe abdominal pain | acute abdomen | appendicitis | sepsis grave | choque septico | fiebre y confusion | dolor abdominal intenso | apendicitis

## Onboard response

1. Escalate suspected infection with new confusion, breathing difficulty or rapid deterioration to the crew medical officer immediately.
2. For severe or rapidly worsening abdominal pain, report the onset, location, associated symptoms and any recent injury or procedure.
3. Share available observations and readings. Keep monitoring the crewmate while the medical officer coordinates assessment and potential mission support.
