# ISS Medical Checklist - ACLS Algorithm (2001)

The historical ISS Medical Checklist dated 6 November 2001 contains an ACLS Algorithm for the International Space Station. The first of six algorithm pages covers CPR (cardiopulmonary resuscitation), patient restraint on the CMRS, and defibrillator equipment. This is a historical reference for questions about emergency protocols and first aid equipment in space. Consult the approved checklist for the current mission for operational use.

Source location: ISS-Medical-Checklist-2001.pdf, PDF page 117, printed page 98, box 6 (CPR).
