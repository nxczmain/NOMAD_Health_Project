### Bacitracin · 500 units/gm, 0.9 gm

Pack and location — Convenience Medication Pack (White) · Spine
Reference quantity — 30 unit dose pack
Possible side effects — No significant side effects

### Loperamide (Imodium) · 2 mg

Pack and location — Convenience Medication Pack (White) · B2
Reference quantity — 40 tablet
Possible side effects — Abdominal discomfort, nausea, vomiting, constipation, drowsiness, dizziness, dry mouth
Reference comments — Use for nonbloody diarrhea. No more than 8 tablets every 24 hours.

### Fexofenadine (Allegra) · 180 mg

Pack and location — Convenience Medication Pack (White) · B2
Reference quantity — 100 tablet
Possible side effects — Headache, vomiting, fatigue, somnolence, dizziness, fever, pain, drowsiness, diarrhea, nausea, upset stomach, muscle aches, back pain, pain in extremities
Reference comments — Do not use with other antihistamines. Do not drink grapefruit juice 1 hour before or 2 hours after taking the medication.

### Loratadine (Claritin) · 10 mg

Pack and location — Convenience Medication Pack (White) · A2
Reference quantity — 375 tablet
Possible side effects — Headache, drowsiness, dizziness, fatigue, dry mouth

### Olopatadine (Pataday) · 0.2 %, 2.5 mL

Pack and location — Convenience Medication Pack (White) · A3
Reference quantity — 6 bottle
Possible side effects — Blurred vision, eye pain, stinging, burning, headache
Reference comments — Remove contact lenses before use. After instilling drops, wait at least 10 minutes before inserting contact lenses.
