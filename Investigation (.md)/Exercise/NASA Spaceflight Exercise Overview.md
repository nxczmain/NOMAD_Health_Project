## PDF page 1

### Purpose of spaceflight exercise

Altered gravity reduces the loading and activity that normally support cardiovascular fitness, muscle function and bone strength. NASA prescribes exercise as one of the countermeasures. Mission duration, gravity and the astronaut's starting fitness influence the type and amount of deconditioning.

## PDF page 11

### Aerobic exercise on the ISS

The ISS uses the CEVIS cycle ergometer and T2 treadmill for steady-state or interval exercise. T2 has a loading system that keeps the astronaut in contact with its belt. The brief describes loads and intensities used by studied crews. Today's exercise prescription and equipment setup come from the crew's assigned plan.

## PDF page 16

### Resistance exercise on ARED

The Advanced Resistive Exercise Device gives the astronaut a load to work against in microgravity. The brief separates squat, heel-raise and deadlift exercise volume in its analysis. Use the trained setup, loading and movement cues for the onboard device.
