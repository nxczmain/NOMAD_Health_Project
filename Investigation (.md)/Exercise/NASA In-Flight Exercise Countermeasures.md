## In-flight equipment and purposes

The presentation illustrates three exercise systems used aboard the International Space Station. T2 treadmill running and CEVIS cycling train aerobic capacity. ARED supplies resistance for muscle and bone loading. The slide titled “In-Flight Exercise Countermeasures” shows the astronauts and the equipment side by side.

The systems support different physiological needs, so a session log should preserve which device and exercise the astronaut used. A program's scheduled minutes and measured outcomes answer different questions.
