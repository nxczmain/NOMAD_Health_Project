### Why compare exercise systems?

Exploration vehicles have limited space and power for exercise equipment. The Zero T2 study compares the nominal ISS combination of T2 treadmill, CEVIS cycling and ARED resistance exercise with CEVIS plus ARED, and with exercise on E4D, an integrated device for aerobic and resistance exercise.

### What the researchers measure

Outcomes include aerobic capacity, muscle strength and endurance, bone mineral density, body composition and sensorimotor measures. The photographs show several of the devices and tests. The study design addresses whether a smaller hardware set can preserve comparable outcomes. The poster describes the investigation and its initial recruitment.
