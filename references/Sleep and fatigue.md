# Sleep and fatigue during spaceflight

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-from-inadequate-sleep-and-irregular-schedules/

Irregular schedules, fragmented sleep, and workload can reduce alertness and performance. The International Space Station uses lighting to support the sleep-wake cycle. Mission staff schedule time for recovery and can adjust fatigue countermeasures for individual duties.

For a check-in, record when you slept, how long you slept, what interrupted it, and whether fatigue affected your work. Report recurring trouble staying alert or a safety concern during duties to the crew medical officer. Follow the mission's schedule and fatigue procedures.
