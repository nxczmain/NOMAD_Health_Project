# Space radiation and acute exposure

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-space-radiation/

Space radiation is a mission hazard. NASA tracks the risk because it depends on the environment, shielding, and exposure. Report a suspected radiation event or a dosimeter alert through mission procedures. Provide exposure timing, location, and measurements if available.

## Acute radiation syndrome

This is a specific illness that can follow a very high penetrating dose received by most or all of the body over a short period. Early symptoms can include nausea, vomiting, headache, and diarrhea. These symptoms are nonspecific, so they do not establish radiation exposure or dose on their own. Do not infer a radiation event from a journal entry or a symptom after surface training. Ask the crew medical officer to evaluate a credible exposure using the event and dose information.

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/signs-symptoms/acute-radiation-syndrome.html
