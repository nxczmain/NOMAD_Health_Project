# First aid for an unresponsive person on Earth

Source: https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/unresponsive-and-breathing-person

Treat unresponsiveness as an emergency and call for help. Check whether the person is breathing normally. If breathing normally, remain with them and watch for changes. Use the recovery position when appropriate and avoid moving someone with a suspected head, neck or back injury unless movement is needed for safety or emergency care.

If breathing is absent or there is only gasping, begin CPR and use an AED as soon as available. Follow the emergency dispatcher's instructions and your training.

This reference describes terrestrial first aid. On a spacecraft or surface mission, activate the onboard emergency procedure and get the crew medical officer immediately. Use vehicle- and mission-specific training for positioning and emergency care.
