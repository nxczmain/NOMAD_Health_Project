# Spaceflight health hazards

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/

NASA groups human spaceflight hazards into radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, changing gravity fields, and hostile or closed environments. Mission stage changes exposure and access to medical support. The same symptom can have different implications in different environments.

The crew log can record when a change began, the mission stage, relevant activities, and measurements already taken. The crew medical officer can interpret trends using the approved mission procedures and the individual's baseline.
